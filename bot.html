How to Connect ▾

1. Enter your bot account's username.
2. Click Get Token ↗ → twitchtokengenerator.com.
3. Log in as the bot account.
4. Tick chat:read and chat:edit.
5. Generate Token → copy Access Token → click 📋 Paste.
6. Enter channel and click Connect.

Chat Commands Reference ▾

!commands — list (30 s cd)
!sounds · !clips — list media (30 s cd)
!timer 5:00 · pause · resume · reset · stop (mod+)
!sound <name> — play a sound
!clip <name> — play a video clip (mod+)
!so @user — shoutout a streamer (mod+)
!wheel — list wheel options · !spin (mod+)
!addcommand · !removecommand (mod+)
!setcooldown <name> <s>
!setpermission <name> <level>

Placeholders:
@user · @target · @targets
@random — random 1–100
@random(min,max) — e.g. @random(1,6)
